## What has not been verified
- **Listening:** nobody has listened to the mixes. Loudness was measured instead (see `EXPORT_MANIFEST.md`).
- **Translations:** they have not been reviewed by native speakers. The seven non-English languages were drafted by an AI model. Store terms match the game's own translation tables, which are also unreviewed.
- **Physical devices:** no phone was used. The footage comes from the iOS Simulator and the Android emulator.
- **Store specifications:** these were read from Apple's and Google's published documentation on 2026-11-27. Re-check them at upload time.
