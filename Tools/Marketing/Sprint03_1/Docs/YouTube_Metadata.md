## AI disclosure ("altered or synthetic content")

Read in YouTube Help on 2026-11-27: creators do not need to disclose content that is clearly unrealistic or animated. The label is for realistic content that could be mistaken for a real person, place or event.

What is in this video:

- The S01–S04 island background comes from the owner's reference illustration, which is **probably AI-generated**. It is stylised and clearly unrealistic.
- Everything else is the game's own art and rig, real gameplay, procedural drawing, and the game's synthesised music. None of it is AI-generated.

Suggested answer: **No** to "altered or synthetic content" (nothing realistic), while keeping the disclosure line already in the description ("The cinematic introduction is stylized promotional artwork…"). If the owner knows the illustration was AI-generated and prefers full transparency, answering **Yes** is also acceptable; it is never wrong to disclose. Re-check the wording in Studio at upload time, because YouTube revises this flow.

Sources: [YouTube Help, disclosing altered or synthetic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en); [YouTube Blog, how creators disclose altered or synthetic content](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content/)
