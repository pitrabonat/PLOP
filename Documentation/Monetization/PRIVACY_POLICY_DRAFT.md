## 1. Summary

PLOP! — Sleepy Puzzle is an offline puzzle game. It has no account, sign-in, chat or user-generated content, and
no in-app purchases.

- **Version A (ads disabled):** the game does not collect or share personal data. Progress and settings stay on your
  device.
- **Version B (ads enabled):** the game shows optional and occasional ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect
  device and usage data to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, as described in section 4.

## 2. Data stored only on your device (both versions)

| Data | Why | Leaves the device? |
|---|---|---|
| Level progress, best moves, Perfect marks | so you can continue | No |
| Dream Dust balance and the cosmetics you unlocked | in-game rewards | No |
| Settings (sound, music, language, haptics, reduced motion, frame rate) | your preferences | No |
| Ad frequency counters (version B): number of levels and minutes since the last ad, ad times within 30 minutes | keeps ads infrequent | No; no identifier is stored |

Uninstalling the game deletes this data. The developer cannot see it or restore it.

## 3. What the developer collects

Nothing. PLOP! has no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, no server and no account system.

## 4. Advertising (version B only)

PLOP! uses **Google AdMob** (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) to show:

- an occasional full-screen ad **between levels**, never during the first 10 levels, never during a puzzle, and at
  most 4 in 30 minutes;
- **optional** rewarded ads you choose to watch for bonus Dream Dust or a free hint.

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process, under Google's own policies:

- **Device identifiers:** on Android, the Android Advertising ID and app set ID. On iOS, PLOP! does **not** ask for
  tracking permission, so the IDFA is not available. Google may use the identifier for vendors (IDFV).
- **IP address and approximate (coarse) location** derived from it.
- **Ad interactions:** ad impressions and taps, and app launches.
- **Diagnostics:** crash and performance data for the SDK.

Purposes: showing and measuring ads, frequency capping, and fraud and security protection. Google acts as an
independent controller for some of this processing. See:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choices**
- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and wherever consent is required, the game shows Google's consent message (User
  Messaging Platform) before any ad request. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings ▸ Privacy
  Choices**.
- Android: you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's Google settings. iOS: tracking stays off
  because PLOP! never asks for it.
- Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rating **G**).

in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).
