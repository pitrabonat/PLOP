# Phase 11 known issues

Open items after Phase 11, by priority. **P0** blocks release, **P1** is a serious gameplay or data problem, **P2** a noticeable quality gap, **P3** polish or a tooling limit. Nothing here is a P0.

| Id | Pri | Area | Issue | Why it is open / next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K-01 | P1 | Validation | **No human playtest.** All difficulty, quality and economy numbers come from heuristics and simulated players. | Run [PLAYTEST_GUIDE.md](PLAYTEST_GUIDE.md) with at least 5 people across experience levels, then recalibrate the bands and `EconomySimulator.Profiles`. |
| K-02 | P1 | Devices | **No physical Android phone or iPhone was available.** The APK was checked on the Android API 37 emulator; iOS on the Simulator. | Install the APK on real hardware. Check touch, frame pacing and safe areas on a notched phone. |
| K-03 | P2 | Performance | Battery and thermal behaviour was **not measured** (neither the emulator nor the Simulator reports meaningful battery or thermal data). | Profile a 20-minute session on a real mid-range phone with the dev overlay (`plop.debug.diagnostics`). |
| K-04 | P2 | Balance | 7 levels are random-solvable (≥ 25 %): 1, 2, 3, 6, 21, 22, 49. All are teaching levels or a mechanic's Discovery/Application, which is intentional, but L22 (Breeze Application) is on the edge (floor 0.3). | Review L22 in a playtest. |
| K-05 | P2 | Balance | 13 levels are flagged `repetitive`: tutorials 2–3, plus 37, 54, 62, 84, 85, 95, 98, 126, 137, 155 and 157, kept or searched puzzles of quality 60–88 where one direction repeats. | The quality score still rates them well. Replace one only if playtesters find its ending rote. |
| K-06 | P2 | Balance | 19 levels are flagged `open` (more than half the floor unused), mostly in Worlds 1–5. | Cosmetic; smaller boards would read better on phones. Not changed, to keep good puzzles' ids. |
| K-07 | P3 | Balance | `easy-for-position` on 10 levels (more than 12 challenge points below their World's median): 7, 22, 36, 37, 38, 51, 87, 92, 138 and 140. Most sit right after a mechanic's Discovery. | Intended as a breather after the new mechanic; 138 and 140 sit late and should be checked in a playtest. |
| K-08 | P3 | Economy | High-Hint players (two hints on most levels) can still be refused the **second** hint on a level when Dust is short (median 29 refusals by level 160). | Grace already guarantees one hint per stuck level. Lower `HintPolicy.GraceAttempts` only if playtests show frustration. |
| K-09 | P3 | Migration | 8 kept boards changed id because their Limited Moves rule changed. A Phase 10 completion carries over only for the 3 whose rule got looser. The other 5 show as new and pay their completion Dust once more (at most 5 × 12 Dust). | Accepted: a stricter rule is a new challenge. |
| K-10 | P3 | Tooling | The quality metrics are heuristics (`PuzzleQuality v1`). Planning depth uses a nest-distance heuristic, and the Limited-information player is a simple model. | Recalibrate weights against playtest data. |
| K-11 | P3 | Tooling | The Level Lab "Quality audit" runs on the main thread with a modal progress bar (about 1 s on an M4 for 160 levels; slower machines will freeze briefly). | Fine for a dev tool. |
| K-13 | P3 | Migration | A migrated Phase 10 player's PLAY button goes to their furthest open level (by design since Phase 8), so the new authored Levels 2 and 4 are skipped unless they open them from Worlds. Seen on the emulator: PLAY → Level 6. | They learned walls in Phase 10 already. Consider a "New levels" badge in Worlds if playtests show players miss them. |
| K-14 | P3 | Build | Gradle's incremental packaging reuses `Library/Bee/…/launcher-debug.apk` and leaves dead space each rebuild (the Phase 10 APK was 64 MB for 46 MB of content; a second Phase 11 build reached 98 MB). Installed size is unaffected. | The final Phase 11 APK was built after deleting that cache file. Delete it before release builds, or let `PlopBuild` do so. |
| K-15 | P3 | Visual | Loop pair 1 shows its single pip in the ring's centre, where it reads as part of the ring until a second pair is on the board. | Fine while pairs differ by tint too; revisit if a colour-blind tester reports confusion. |
| K-12 | P3 | Visual | Loop pairs beyond 6 in one level would repeat both tint and pip count. No shipped level has more than 2 pairs. | `QualityTarget.MaxTiles` keeps shipped boards far below that. |

Fixed during Phase 11 (see [QA_RESULTS.md](QA_RESULTS.md)): trivial opening; the Frost start at L7; decorative mechanics; mis-scaled Limited Moves slack; a Daily pool without mechanics; no hint recourse when out of Dust; colour-only Loop pairs; tiny fan pips; a kept puzzle judged by its old slot (Level 11, quality 29 → replaced); a stray `ProjectSettings.asset` preload left by an interrupted build (reverted).
